Privacy Policy

Ghost of Me · last updated 8 November 2026

Ghost of Me does not collect any personal data.

The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Your progress and settings (which rooms you have finished, the photos taken in the game, language and sound settings) are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game deletes them.

If you write to us by email, we use your message and address only to answer you, and we do not share them.

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from children.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: ecansubasoglu@gmail.com.
