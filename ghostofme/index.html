Ghost of Me

A calm puzzle game about working together with your own past.

An old painter is forgetting. Every room is a fragment of a life, floating in the dark. Rewind, and a coral echo of your past repeats everything you just did. Your past can hold a door open. Only you can walk through.

Free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected.
